# Case study format

Case pages follow Problem → Approach → Impact spine plus optional sections used conditionally: Context, Who we designed for, Constraints, Key decisions, Tech stack, Learnings. Flavourful names ("Modelling the domain", "Research") live at h3 level inside Approach, never as h2s. Impact has three registers — measured numbers, structural results (a decision or commitment the work caused), or intent ("what the design targets", for engagements too short or old to validate); pick one deliberately and never force a metric onto direction work. See `cases/template.html` for section order and guidance comments.

`Context` holds the forces that shaped the problem — business position, technology situation, the ecosystem the work landed in. `Constraints` is narrower and harder — lifecycle, scale, environment, org limits that closed off options.

## Choosing a shape

Shape is chosen per section, from a pair of alternatives, rather than picked once as a style for the whole page. Each pair below asks a single question about what the work actually produced, and the answer settles that section's shape. Every alternative runs the same meta-sequence: an overview showing the whole set at once, then one h3 per member, each member built the same way. Adopt the sequence, not just the name — and note that the overview is the move most easily skipped, because by the time you're writing you already hold the set in your head.
