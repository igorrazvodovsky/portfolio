My work centres on knowledge representation — making it explicit enough to steer and ground AI systems. Sometimes that knowledge is a product's domain: its concepts, tasks, and user needs. Sometimes it's design practice itself: the patterns and judgement behind how I work. Currently I'm exploring this through agentic conceptual modelling, product discovery automation, and automated UI generation.

Most recently, I worked on industrial sales automation at Tacton. Before that, I was at Kuehne+Nagel, where I designed everyday workflow tools for employees.

As an individual contributor, I'm strongest early: conceptual modelling, uncovering user needs, exploring directions, and turning strategy into concrete design. I work by making — prototypes and quick experiments.
As a team collaborator, I shape how design happens: fitting design work into the development cycle, building design systems and reusable patterns, and making reflection and critique part of everyday work.
AI cuts across both — as a tool reshaping how I research, prototype, and synthesise, and as a material reshaping what products can fundamentally do for users.
